The solution

One platform. Three real flows.

Whatever currency you have, or whatever currency you're owed — Beam handles the conversion.

Instant payout

Get paid in crypto or USD — cash out in your own currency in minutes.

Pay abroad

Holding naira in Lagos, buying something in Accra? Pay in what you have — they get cedis, automatically.

Escrow for hiring

A client funds the job in crypto. Beam holds it until work is done, then releases it in one click.
